# India's Net Zero Transition

**Global physics → India reality → Energy Group opportunity**

A report for an ordinary Indian reader on why the energy transition is physically hard, built on McKinsey Global Institute's "physical challenges" work and rebuilt on Indian data. It ends in two versions: a national conclusion (general audience) and the Energy Group opportunity (Energy Group edition).

It is written as a chain of simple questions and answers, grouped into 8 chapters, in a warm, playful "curious detective" voice.
