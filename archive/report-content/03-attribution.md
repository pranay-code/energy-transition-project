Mostly us — and that is exactly why we call it "human-induced" climate change. But how can we be so
sure it is people and not just nature doing its thing? Well, look at the timing. For thousands of
years the air stayed more or less steady, and it only started heating up once we began running
factories, power plants and cars everywhere. The amounts match too: the extra carbon now floating in
the air is about the same as all the fuel we have dug up and burned. And nature has an alibi — the
Sun isn't any hotter, and volcanoes and oceans are behaving just as they always have. So all the
clues point back to us.

*Sources: IPCC AR6 WG1 Ch.3 (attribution); Global Carbon Project, 2024.*
