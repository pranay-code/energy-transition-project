# Q6 — Since this is a world issue, did governments come together and try to fix it? Any solutions or plans?

Yes — and they've been at it for over thirty years. Way back in 1992, countries met at the Earth
Summit in Rio and agreed, for the first time, that they should tackle this together. Then came a
string of big meetings. In 1997 the Kyoto deal asked rich countries to cut their emissions, but it
left out the poorer ones, and some of the biggest polluters never really joined. In 2009 a huge
summit in Copenhagen was meant to fix all that — and it mostly fell apart.

So for years there was plenty of talking and not much progress. Every country worried that cutting
back would hurt its own growth while the others carried on. Then, in 2015, they finally landed on
something that stuck: a deal signed in Paris that nearly every nation in the world agreed to. That
deal — the Paris Agreement — is where the real story of action begins.

*Sources: UNFCCC (Rio 1992, Kyoto 1997, Copenhagen 2009, Paris 2015); IEA.*
